Run-apalooza Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Run-apalooza keeps your runs and health data on your iPhone and Apple Watch. There's no account, and the app has no servers, analytics, ads, or tracking. Your running data only leaves your devices when you use the built-in coach and have allowed it to go to Anthropic, when you turn on Jev checks and allow it to go to TypeSafe, or when you share something yourself.

What the app uses

  • Location, during runs only: to measure distance and pace, draw your route, and look up the weather where you ran.
  • Apple Health: the app reads your running workouts, heart rate, steps, and weight, and saves your runs as workouts. It uses them to show your history, heart-rate zones, cadence, and calories.
  • Motion & Fitness: your steps and the barometer, for cadence and elevation during runs.
  • Your music library, if you turn on music: to play a playlist during runs.
  • What you enter: run notes and effort ratings, your coach profile (goal race, background, injuries, schedule), training plans, and chats with the coach.
  • Your Claude API key, if you use the built-in coach.
  • Your TypeSafe API key, if you turn on Jev checks.

Where it's kept

On your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch during runs. Runs, plans, notes, and chats are stored in the app. Your Claude and TypeSafe API keys are stored in the iPhone's Keychain and only sent to Anthropic and TypeSafe, respectively. Nothing is stored in iCloud.

What leaves your devices

To Anthropic, only with your permission. The built-in coach runs on Claude, made by Anthropic. The app asks before sending anything. If you allow it, then when you check in, start a new conversation, write a plan with the coach, or summarize your history, it sends Anthropic:

  • your runs: date, distance, time, pace, climbing, and weather
  • from Apple Health: heart rate, time in heart-rate zones, cadence, and calories
  • your effort ratings and run notes
  • your coach profile, the coach's notes, your training plan, and your chat messages

Your route and location aren't sent. Requests go over an encrypted connection with your own API key, and Anthropic handles them under its privacy policy. You can turn sharing off at any time in Coach → Coach Settings → Share running data with Anthropic. The coach can't answer while it's off.

To TypeSafe, only if you turn on Jev checks and allow it. Jev is a decision model made by TypeSafe AI. It's off unless you turn it on in Coach → Coach Settings, and the app asks before sending anything. It answers with a pick from a short list, and gets only what each check needs:

  • plan check: this week's planned workouts, and the distance, time, pace, and average heart rate of the week's runs
  • run check: a run's distance, time, pace, pauses, average heart rate and cadence, and GPS signal counts
  • problem check: what you wrote in a problem report, the run's distance, time, and pace, its GPS and heart-rate counts, and the app's event log
  • injury check: your weekly distance, and recent runs' distance, pace, effort, and notes

Your route, location, coach profile, and chats aren't sent. Requests go over an encrypted connection with your own TypeSafe API key, and TypeSafe handles them under its privacy policy. Answers are kept on your iPhone so the same numbers aren't sent twice. Turn it off any time in Coach Settings → Share running data with TypeSafe; the checks stop and the saved answers are deleted.

To Apple. Runs are saved to Apple Health, which follows your Health settings. To show the weather, the app sends the location where your run started to Apple Weather. Music plays through Apple Music.

When you share something yourself. If you share a check-in to the Claude app, or share a problem report (which can include your route if you choose), it goes wherever you send it.

Your health and fitness data is never sold, and never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining.

Your choices

  • Stop sharing with Anthropic: Coach → Coach Settings → Share running data with Anthropic. This stops anything else being sent. Your Anthropic account and Anthropic's privacy policy cover what it already received.
  • Stop sharing with TypeSafe: Coach → Coach Settings → Share running data with TypeSafe, or turn off Jev checks.
  • Health access: Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Run-apalooza.
  • Delete your data: delete runs in History, or chats in Coach → Profile & Notes. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Workouts it saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. If the app starts sending Anthropic or TypeSafe anything new, it asks you again first.
