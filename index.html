Run-apalooza

A running app for iPhone and Apple Watch: GPS runs, Apple Health, training plans, and a running coach.

Support

Questions, problems, or ideas: run-apalooza@proton.me.

If a run recorded wrong, open it in History and tap Report a Problem with This Run. It makes a report with the run's technical details (not your route, unless you include it) that you can email to the address above.

Privacy

Your runs stay on your iPhone and in Apple Health. Read the privacy policy.
